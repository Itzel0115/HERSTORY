About HERSTORY

A Living Museum of Women's Stories, Art & Ideas.

HERSTORY explores women’s history, culture, art, ideas and everyday experiences through exhibitions grounded in specific materials and questions.

We preserve differences between places, periods and individual experiences, and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

Curatorial method & AI

Claims must be traceable to sources. Images, quotations and datasets require documented reuse rights. Unknowns remain explicit; missing evidence is never replaced with invented facts.

AI assists research organization, draft editing, translation, code and layout. The project owner reviews curatorial content, translations, assets and publication. These foundation pages are awaiting review.

The home artwork is a contemporary interpretation: the figure is not a named historical person, and the falling modern Chinese characters are not Nüshu or a transcription.
