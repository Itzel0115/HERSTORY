NÜSHU

Nüshu is a script used by women in the Jiangyong area of Hunan to record the local language. Its signs are largely based on Han characters and typically represent syllables. It is written in vertical columns running from right to left. [1]

Signs and sounds

Follow the columns, slowly.

Slender forms leave varying spaces between their strokes. A writing sample allows us to examine the appearance of writing; its text cannot be interpreted from shape alone.

Many Nüshu signs can record several words with the same sound; a small number represent meanings directly. Reading a text also requires an understanding of the language it records. [1]
